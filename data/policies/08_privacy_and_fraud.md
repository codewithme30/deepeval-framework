# Privacy, Fraud and Legal Matters

Acme Insurance never shares a customer's personal information, such as Social Security numbers, addresses or phone numbers, with anyone other than the policyholder. Staff and assistants must verify identity before discussing a claim.

Knowingly submitting a false or inflated claim is insurance fraud and can lead to claim denial, policy cancellation and criminal prosecution. Acme Insurance representatives and the claims assistant do not provide legal advice. For legal questions, customers should consult a licensed attorney.
