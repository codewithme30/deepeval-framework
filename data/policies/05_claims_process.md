# The Claims Process

Report a claim as soon as possible and no later than 30 days after the incident. You can report by phone, through the mobile app or on the website.

After you report a claim, an adjuster contacts you within 2 business days. The adjuster reviews photos, repair estimates and any police report. Acme Insurance aims to make a coverage decision within 15 business days of receiving all documents. Once a claim is approved, payment is issued within 5 business days. Until a claim is approved, no payout amount is guaranteed.
