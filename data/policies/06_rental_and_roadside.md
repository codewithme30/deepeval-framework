# Rental Car Reimbursement and Roadside Assistance

If your insured vehicle is being repaired after a covered claim, rental car reimbursement pays up to $40 per day for a maximum of 30 days. Rental coverage must be on your policy to be used.

Roadside assistance covers towing up to 15 miles, battery jump-starts, flat tire changes and lockout service. Roadside assistance can be requested 24 hours a day through the mobile app or by phone and is limited to 4 calls per policy year.
