# Cancellation and Renewal

You may cancel your policy at any time. Acme Insurance requires 30 days written notice to cancel for non-payment of premium or to non-renew a policy. Premiums paid for unused coverage are refunded pro rata.

Policies renew automatically every 12 months. A grace period of 10 days applies after a missed premium payment; coverage continues during the grace period but lapses if payment is not received by its end.
