# Deductibles

A deductible is the amount you pay out of pocket on a covered claim before Acme Insurance pays the rest. The payout is the claim amount minus the deductible.

The standard deductible for auto collision and comprehensive claims is $500. A lower deductible of $250 is available on some auto policies for a higher premium. The standard deductible for home claims is $1,000. Wind and hail damage to a home carries a separate deductible of 2% of the dwelling limit. No deductible applies to liability claims.
