# Home Insurance Coverage

Acme Insurance home policies protect the dwelling, other structures such as fences and sheds, and personal belongings. Covered events include fire, lightning, windstorm, hail, theft, vandalism and sudden water damage from burst pipes.

Standard home policies carry a dwelling limit of $350,000 and personal property coverage of 50% of the dwelling limit. Additional living expenses are paid for up to 12 months if your home cannot be lived in after a covered loss.
