# Auto Insurance Coverage

Acme Insurance auto policies include three coverages. Liability coverage pays for injury and property damage you cause to others, up to the limit shown on your policy. Collision coverage pays to repair your own vehicle after an accident, whether or not you were at fault. Comprehensive coverage pays for non-collision damage such as theft, vandalism, fire, falling objects and hail.

Standard auto policies carry a coverage limit of $50,000 per claim. Uninsured motorist protection is included on every auto policy at no extra charge.
