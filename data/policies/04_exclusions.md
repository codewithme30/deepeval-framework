# Exclusions: What Is Not Covered

The following are not covered by auto or home policies: flood damage, earthquake damage, normal wear and tear, damage you cause on purpose, and losses from war or nuclear hazards.

Auto policies also exclude vehicles used for racing and vehicles used for commercial delivery or ride-sharing unless a commercial endorsement has been added. Home policies exclude damage caused by pests, mold from long-term neglect and sewer backup unless an endorsement has been added. Flood insurance is available separately through a government-backed program.
